# Sheets product mark

`src/assets/sheets-2026.svg` reproduces the Sheets 2026 product mark from the public Google Sheets interface, observed on 2026-10-19.

Source: https://ssl.gstatic.com/docs/common/material_common_sprite1052_gm3_grey_medium.svg

The original group at `translate(1328 298)` is extracted without its sprite translation and placed in a 36 × 36 view box. The paths, colors, gradient, mask and filter are preserved. The Google Sheets product mark belongs to Google LLC; it is separate from the Apache-2.0 Material Symbols collection and from the application's GPL-3.0-only source code.
